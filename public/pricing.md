# Pricing — MoneySpent

MoneySpent is free forever for personal use. Built for people, not subscription pressure.

## Personal
- Price: $0 / ₹0 per month
- Billing: Free forever for personal use
- Credit card: Not required
- Limits: Unlimited manual transactions, multiple accounts & cards
- Features:
  - Expense and income tracking
  - Smart category management
  - Spending insights
  - Account balance tracking
  - Private personal data
  - No ads, no tracking spam
  - Web app / PWA access
